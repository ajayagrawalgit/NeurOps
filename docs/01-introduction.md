# 🌟 Introduction to NeurOps

Welcome to **NeurOps**, the AI-Powered Infrastructure Observability & Chaos Engineering platform.

## 🚀 Mission
The mission of NeurOps is to bridge the gap between raw hardware telemetry and intelligent operational decision-making. In a world of hyperscale infrastructure, manual monitoring is no longer sufficient. NeurOps uses **Agentic AI** to observe, analyze, and stabilize systems automatically.

## 💡 What is this?
NeurOps is a full-stack ecosystem designed to:
1.  **Simulate** real-world server hardware using Redfish standards.
2.  **Infect** systems with synthetic failures (Chaos Engineering) to test resilience.
3.  **Observe** anomalies and trends in real-time through intelligent telemetry collection.
4.  **Assis** operators via a specialized AI Assistant that understands infrastructure context.

## 🤖 Why it exists
Modern data centers generate petabytes of telemetry data. Traditional dashboards (Grafana, etc.) tell you *what* is happening, but they don't always tell you *why* or *what to do*.

NeurOps exists to provide **Actionable Intelligence**. By integrating Google's Gemini models directly into the infrastructure loop, we turn metrics into insights and insights into actions.
