# 🌟 Introduction to NeurOps

Welcome to **NeurOps**, the enterprise-ready Infrastructure Intelligence & Operational Resilience platform.

## 🚀 Mission
The mission of NeurOps is to transform raw hardware telemetry into actionable operational intelligence. In hyperscale environments, traditional monitoring is no longer sufficient; NeurOps leverages **Agentic AI** to observe, analyze, and stabilize infrastructure with production-scale efficiency.

## 💡 What is this?
NeurOps is a full-stack product suite designed to:
1.  **Monitor** real-world server hardware using industry-standard Redfish APIs.
2.  **Validate** resilience using a built-in Chaos Engineering engine for synthetic failure injection.
3.  **Analyze** anomalies and trends in real-time through high-fidelity telemetry pipelines.
4.  **Assis** operators via a specialized AI Assistant that deep-links infrastructure telemetry with root-cause insights.

## 🤖 Why it exists
Modern data centers generate petabytes of telemetry data. Traditional dashboards (Grafana, etc.) tell you *what* is happening, but they don't always tell you *why* or *what to do*.

NeurOps exists to provide **Actionable Intelligence**. By integrating Google's Gemini models directly into the infrastructure loop, we turn metrics into insights and insights into actions.
